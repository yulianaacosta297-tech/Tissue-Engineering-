# **RESULTS**

## 2D Model Results

The control group showed typical HeLa morphology, good adhesion, and high cell density. H₂O₂ induced concentration-dependent morphological damage, including cell rounding and reduced cell–cell contact. NAC 5 mM provided greater morphological protection than 1 mM, while NAC-only controls showed no evident cytotoxic effects.

The DCFH-DA assay showed a non-linear response, with the control displaying the highest fluorescence. Most H₂O₂-treated groups showed reduced fluorescence, likely due to compromised membrane integrity and reduced probe retention. G9 (5 mM NAC \+ 150 μM H₂O₂) showed residual DCF fluorescence, suggesting partial preservation of cell viability while oxidative stress remained present.

## 3D Spheroid Model Results

After 48 h on 1% agar, HeLa cells formed visible spheroidal aggregates in all experimental wells, confirming successful 3D aggregation. The spheroids showed irregular to semi-spherical morphology with moderate size variability.

No detectable DCFH-DA fluorescence was observed across the experimental groups, including H₂O₂-treated controls. The spheroids exposed to 250 μM H₂O₂ maintained their structural integrity, unlike the morphological damage observed in the 2D model under similar conditions.

These findings suggest that the 3D spheroid model exhibited greater intrinsic resistance to oxidative stress, potentially due to multicellular organization and diffusion-related effects.
