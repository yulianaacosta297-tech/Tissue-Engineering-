# Protective Effect of N-Acetylcysteine Against Oxidative Stress in 2D and 3D HeLa Cell Models

## Project Summary

Oxidative stress is a cellular damage mechanism involved in diseases such as cancer, diabetes, neurodegenerative disorders, and chronic inflammatory conditions. N-acetylcysteine (NAC) is an antioxidant used due to its ability to increase intracellular glutathione (GSH) levels and neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS).

This project evaluated the cytoprotective effect of NAC against hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂)-induced oxidative stress in HeLa cells using three experimental models of increasing complexity:

- **2D monolayer culture**
- **3D spheroids**
- **3D alginate hydrogels**

NAC concentrations of 1 mM and 5 mM were evaluated, while oxidative stress was induced using H₂O₂. Intracellular ROS were assessed using DCFH-DA, while metabolic activity in alginate hydrogels was evaluated using resazurin.

The results demonstrated that cellular architecture significantly influenced the response to oxidative stress. In 2D cultures, 5 mM NAC showed a greater protective effect than 1 mM. The 3D spheroid model showed no detectable DCF signal, suggesting greater resistance to oxidative stress. In the alginate hydrogel model, H₂O₂ reduced metabolic activity to **31.4% of the control**, while NAC restored it to **95.4%**.

Overall, the findings highlight the importance of 3D models for evaluating cytoprotective responses and demonstrate the potential relevance of NAC in tissue engineering, toxicology, and drug screening applications.

## Research Question

**How does the cellular architecture of 2D and 3D culture models influence the cytoprotective effect of N-acetylcysteine against oxidative stress?**

## Experimental Models

| Model | Description | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| 2D | HeLa cell monolayer | DCFH-DA |
| 3D | HeLa spheroids | DCFH-DA |
| 3D | Alginate hydrogel | Resazurin |

## Experimental Design

### NAC concentrations

- 1 mM
- 5 mM

### Oxidative stress

- H₂O₂

### Analytical methods

- DCFH-DA fluorescence assay
- Resazurin metabolic activity assay
- Morphological analysis

## Key Findings

### 2D Monolayer

H₂O₂ induced dose-dependent morphological changes. The 5 mM NAC treatment showed a greater protective effect compared with 1 mM NAC.

### 3D Spheroids

No detectable DCF fluorescence was observed, suggesting increased resistance to oxidative stress potentially associated with multicellular organization and diffusion gradients.

### 3D Alginate Hydrogel

H₂O₂ reduced metabolic activity to **31.4%** of the untreated control.

NAC treatment restored metabolic activity to **95.4%**.

## Conclusions

The results demonstrate that the dimensionality and architecture of the cellular model can significantly influence the response to oxidative stress.

The 3D spheroid model showed the greatest apparent resistance to oxidative stress, while the alginate hydrogel provided the strongest functional evidence of NAC-mediated cytoprotection.

These findings support the use of physiologically relevant 3D models in **tissue engineering, toxicology, and drug screening**.
